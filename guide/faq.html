Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about Karkhanis Labs and our products
What is Karkhanis Labs?
Karkhanis Labs builds modern software tools that simplify shared financial coordination and collaboration.
What products does Karkhanis Labs develop?
Karkhanis Labs develops multiple software applications. Each product has its own guide and documentation available under the Guide section.
Does Karkhanis Labs process payments?
Our applications help coordinate and track transactions between users. Actual payments occur through third-party payment providers such as UPI or bank transfers.
Where can I find guides for specific products?
Each product has its own documentation inside the Guide section. For example, the Traxettle guide explains how to manage shared expenses.
