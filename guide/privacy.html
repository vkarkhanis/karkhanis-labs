Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026

Traxettle is a product developed and operated by Karkhanis Labs. This Privacy Policy explains how information is collected, used and protected when using the Traxettle platform.

Information We Collect

Account Information

  • Name
  • Email address
  • Profile information you provide

Usage Information

  • Events created
  • Expenses recorded
  • Group participation information

Technical Information

  • IP address
  • Browser type
  • Device information

Traxettle does not store bank account numbers or card details.

How We Use Information

  • Provide expense tracking services
  • Synchronize shared event data
  • Improve reliability and performance
  • Provide customer support

Payments

Traxettle does not process or store payments directly. Participants may settle balances using third-party payment providers such as UPI or bank transfers outside the platform.

Data Sharing

We do not sell or rent personal information. Information may be shared only with participants in the same event or when required by law.

Data Security

Traxettle uses encrypted communication and reasonable security practices to protect information. However no system can guarantee complete security.

Changes to this Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated periodically. Updates will be published on this page.
